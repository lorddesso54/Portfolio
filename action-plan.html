My Career Action Plan

Career goal: Become a software engineer by building a strong foundation in my first year of senior high school, completing TechPro ICT, considering a BSCS degree, and gaining experience step by step.

My next steps

My goals, actions & timeline

This plan starts from where I am now as a first-year senior high school student. The stages use school years rather than fixed calendar dates, so I can adjust them to my school's schedule, college admission dates, and the program I choose.

Stage What I Need Action I Will Take Timeline
Build my foundation Consistent study habits, core school subjects, and support from my IT teachers and advisers. Complete my first-year SHS subjects, strengthen my computer and programming basics, and ask teachers for feedback. Keep practicing with small exercises alongside schoolwork. First year SHS: now through the end of the school year
Complete senior high school TechPro ICT coursework, a stronger portfolio, and a plan for college applications and costs. Complete my second SHS year, improve a few projects, and research BSCS programs, admission requirements, tuition, and scholarships with my family and school adviser. Second year SHS, including the months before graduation
Start a BSCS degree Admission to a suitable program, steady study habits, and foundations in programming, mathematics, and computing. If I meet the requirements and the plan is practical for my family, enroll in BSCS. Focus first on foundational courses and gradually build programming projects and teamwork experience. After SHS: typically college years 1–2; confirm the chosen program's duration
Prepare for employment More advanced coursework, completed degree requirements, practical projects, and professional communication. Complete my degree requirements, work on collaborative projects, seek internships or other relevant experience when available, and prepare a portfolio and résumé. Later college years, then as I approach graduation
Begin my career A completed qualification, a clear portfolio, interview preparation, and willingness to keep learning. Apply for suitable entry-level software roles, learn from feedback, and continue building my skills on the job. After graduation; timing depends on my studies and opportunities

What will help me follow through

Resources & accountability

Resources I can use

My preparation can draw on TechPro ICT coursework, advice from teachers and school advisers, beginner programming lessons, and practice projects. Comparing college program details and financial support options will help me plan for BSCS.

How I will measure progress

At the end of each school term, I will review coursework, programming practice, project evidence, and next-step requirements. If a task becomes difficult, I will break it into smaller steps and seek guidance.
