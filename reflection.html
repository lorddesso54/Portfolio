Learner Reflection

Looking back on the process of exploring my career direction and planning the next steps.

What I discovered

My reflection

What did I discover about myself while creating my Career Portfolio?

I discovered that I am especially interested in how technology can be used to solve problems. Creating this portfolio helped me organize a large career goal into smaller areas: education, skills, project work, and next steps.

Which personal, social, or environmental factor has the greatest influence on my career decision? Why?

The academic pathway has the greatest influence on my decision because it shapes the preparation I can take now and the opportunities I can pursue later. TechPro ICT is my chosen senior-high path, and a BSCS degree is the college direction I am considering.

What skills do I already possess that can help me in my chosen career?

Curiosity, patience, and problem-solving are useful strengths for my chosen career. The work represented in this portfolio also shows how clear organization, readable information, and attention to how pages connect can make a digital project easier to use.

What is the first step I will take toward achieving my career goal?

My first step is to prepare for TechPro ICT by reviewing the program expectations and strengthening my computer and problem-solving fundamentals. I will consider this step complete when I have a regular study routine and understand the subjects I will focus on.

What skills do I still need to develop?

I still need to develop programming fluency, debugging, version control, and software design. I also want to grow my confidence in explaining technical ideas and collaborating with others. Coursework, regular practice, and increasingly challenging projects will help me build these skills.

A note to my future self

As I continue through TechPro ICT and explore BSCS programs, I will return to these goals, add new project evidence, and adjust my plan based on what I learn.
