People & place

My Career Influences

My career choice is shaped by the people who guide me and by the educational and technological opportunities around me. These influences help me plan a practical path toward software engineering.

People who shape my thinking

My support and role models

Family & friends

I come from a family that has spent a lot of time around technology, and we used to run a computer shop. Being around computers helped make technology a familiar part of my life and encouraged my interest in how it works. Many of my peers are also interested in technology and programming, so we can share ideas, learn from one another, and motivate each other as I work toward a career in software engineering.

Teachers & mentors

My teachers and school advisers have helped me a lot as I think about my future and the steps I need to take. My IT teachers have especially encouraged my interest in technology and helped me learn more about computers and programming. Their support motivates me to keep improving and work toward my goal of becoming a software engineer.

Role models

My IT teachers and the YouTube creators I watch are role models who inspire me to learn more about technology. My teachers support my learning in class, while creators share projects, programming tips, and ideas that help me see what I can build. They motivate me to keep practicing and work toward becoming a software engineer.

Support & challenges

Social & environmental factors

Location

Where I live and study

Location can affect my access to education and future work. Living near a city may provide more schools and software-related job opportunities, while living farther away can make them harder to reach. I will explore the study and career options available to me as I work toward becoming a software engineer.

Finances

Costs & resources

Studying software engineering can involve costs for education and devices. These expenses may be a challenge, so I will plan carefully and make use of free online learning resources to build my skills.

Technology

Access & change

Technology supports my learning because practicing coding requires access to a computer and the internet. I can use these tools, along with online lessons and practice activities, to keep developing my programming skills.

Employment

Opportunities & competition

There are many job opportunities for software engineers, but competition for roles can also be high. I will keep learning, strengthen my programming skills, and build project experience to prepare for future opportunities.
