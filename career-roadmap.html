Where I'm headed

Career Road Map

I am currently in my first year of senior high school and plan to take the TechPro ICT pathway. My next steps are to complete SHS, consider a BSCS degree, and build the skills and experience needed for a software engineering career. The timing below is a guide and can change based on school calendars, admission requirements, and opportunities.

What the career asks for

Academic & skills requirements

Academic preparation

  • Senior high school: TechPro ICT is my chosen pathway for developing practical information and communications technology skills.
  • College: I plan to pursue a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (BSCS), subject to admission requirements and available opportunities.
  • Important areas of study include programming, algorithms, data structures, databases, operating systems, and software design.
  • Employers may also value practical experience, communication, teamwork, and a portfolio of completed work.

Technical & professional skills

  • Gain fluency in at least one programming language and learn to write, test, debug, and maintain code.
  • Practice Git, collaboration workflows, documentation, and foundational software design.
  • Develop communication, teamwork, adaptability, and a habit of learning from feedback.

Career timeline

Steps toward the goal

  1. NOW · SHS YEAR 1

    Finish my first year of senior high school

    I will focus on my current subjects, strengthen basic computer and programming skills, and seek help or feedback from my IT teachers and advisers. I will keep practice manageable alongside my schoolwork.

    Milestone: Complete the first-year requirements and keep a few clear examples of what I have learned.

  2. NEXT · SHS YEAR 2

    Complete senior high school and plan for college

    I will complete my TechPro ICT coursework, continue improving small projects, and check BSCS admission requirements, program costs, and possible financial support with my family and school adviser.

    Milestone: Graduate from SHS and make an informed decision about where and how to apply for college.

  3. AFTER SHS · COLLEGE

    Pursue a BSCS degree if it is the right path for me

    If accepted and financially practical, I will study computer science, starting with programming and other foundations before moving into more advanced topics. I will build projects and seek teamwork or internship opportunities when available. Program length depends on the institution.

    Milestone: Progress through the program and complete its academic and practical requirements.

  4. AFTER GRADUATION

    Apply for entry-level software roles and keep learning

    After completing my chosen education path, I will prepare a portfolio and résumé, apply for suitable entry-level roles, and continue improving through practical work and feedback.

    Milestone: Begin gaining professional experience and keep developing my skills.
